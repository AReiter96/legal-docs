## 2. Overview

The app calculates how much material a home improvement project needs (paint,
wallpaper, tiles). It works without an account or registration. We do not run
any servers of our own: everything you enter stays on your device. The app is
funded by advertising from Google AdMob (section 5).

## 3. Data on your device

The app stores projects, rooms, measurements, material choices, prices and your
settings (language, units, currency) in a database on your device. We do not
receive this data and have no access to it.

The data is kept until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data in the
Android settings, or uninstall the app.

## 4. Android backup

Android can back up app data and transfer it to a new device. The app takes part
in this with exactly one file: the database from section 3.

- If Google backup is turned on for your device, Android stores this file in
  your Google account. The backup is made by Android and Google, not by us. We
  have no access to it.
- When you move to a new device, Android can transfer the file directly.

You decide whether and how backups are made in the Android settings (usually
under "Google" → "Backup"). The backup is covered by
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 5. Advertising via Google AdMob

The app shows ads via Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon
House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Google may process in
particular:

- your device's advertising ID,
- your IP address,
- information about the device (such as device type and Android version),
- interactions with ads (such as whether an ad was shown or tapped).

We do not see this data ourselves, only aggregated statistics that do not
identify you, such as how many ads were served.

### Consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks
on first launch, through a dialog provided by Google (User Messaging Platform),
whether you consent to personalised ads and the access to your device they
require. Without consent, the app shows only non-personalised or limited ads.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time: in the ⋮ menu at the top
right, under **Ad settings**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID
in the Android settings (usually under "Google" → "Ads", or "Privacy" → "Ads").

### Legal basis

Personalised ads and access to the advertising ID are based on your consent
(Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, Section 25(1) TDDDG). Non-personalised ads are based on our
legitimate interest in offering the app free of charge and funding it through
advertising (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Personalised ads rely on profiling by Google. No automated decision with legal
effect on you takes place.

Google may also transfer data to countries outside the EU, such as the United
States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, so such
transfers are covered by the European Commission's adequacy decision of 10 July
2023. For details, see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its
services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 6. Rating on Google Play

After an export, the app asks for a rating at most once, using the Google Play
rating dialog. Google Play provides this dialog; you submit a rating directly to
Google, and we do not receive any data from you in the process.

## 7. PDF export and sharing

The app creates PDFs on your device. If you share one using Android's share
function, for example by email or messenger, the file leaves your device through
your own action. We are not involved and receive no copy.

## 8. No analytics or crash reporting services

The app contains no analytics or crash reporting services of its own. Google
Play provides us with aggregated crash and performance statistics (Android
vitals) if you have agreed to share diagnostic data on your device. These
statistics do not identify you.

## 9. No sharing with other third parties

Apart from Google as described in sections 4 to 6 and 8, we do not share any
data.

## 10. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right of access
(Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing
(Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). You can withdraw
consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)), see section 5.

Since we do not store any data about you ourselves, these rights mainly concern
processing by Google. If you have questions, contact us at the email address in
section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory
authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

You are under no legal or contractual obligation to provide us with data.
Without consent to personalised ads, you can still use the app in full.
